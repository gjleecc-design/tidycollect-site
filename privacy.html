點滴相簿 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日　｜　適用 App：點滴相簿（英文名稱 TidyCollect）

一句話版本：你的照片、連結、想法和整理紀錄只存在你的手機裡。我們沒有伺服器、不要求註冊、不收集任何個人資料，也不使用廣告或分析工具。

1. 我們收集什麼

什麼都不收集。點滴相簿沒有帳號系統，也沒有內建任何第三方 SDK（廣告、分析、當機回報、追蹤）。開發者無法看到你的照片、你的整理結果，也無法知道你如何使用 App。

2. App 會在你的手機上處理什麼

資料用途存放位置
照片與影片（透過 iOS 的「照片」權限）顯示、分類、加入相簿、標示最愛、編輯、壓縮、找出重複，以及依你的操作刪除仍在系統「照片」資料庫，App 不另外上傳
整理紀錄（保留、刪除、待處理標記、統計、獎章）記住你整理到哪裡App 的私有儲存空間
連結、想法（文字、拍照、相片、影片、繪畫）與分類你自己建立的收藏與筆記App 的私有儲存空間；分享選單的擴充功能與主程式透過 App Group 共用同一份
設定（外觀、語言、鎖定相簿清單、暱稱）保存你的偏好App 的私有儲存空間

以上資料都留在你的裝置上，我們不會取得，也不會傳送到任何伺服器。刪除 App 後，App 內的資料會一併移除（照片資料庫裡的照片不受影響，除非你當初在 App 內刪除了它們）。

3. 權限說明

  • 照片：讀取照片才能整理；加入相簿、設定最愛、編輯與刪除，會依你的操作修改你的照片資料庫。你可以在「設定 › 點滴相簿 › 照片」改成只開放部分照片。
  • 相機：只在你於「想法」選擇「拍照」時使用，拍好的相片存進 App 變成一張圖卡。
  • 麥克風與語音辨識：只在你使用「紀錄」的「麥克風隨手記」時錄音並轉成文字。錄音以壓縮檔存在你的手機內。語音轉文字由 iOS 的語音辨識執行；依語言與裝置不同，音訊可能被傳送到 Apple 伺服器處理，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。本 App 不會把錄音傳到我們的伺服器。
  • Face ID／Touch ID：用於解鎖你上鎖的相簿。驗證由 iOS 系統完成，App 不會取得你的臉部或指紋資料。

4. 網路連線

點滴相簿本身不連線到任何我們營運的伺服器。只有在你主動儲存連結時，你的手機才會直接連到你儲存的那個網站，用來取得網頁標題、預覽圖，或在你選擇「整頁封存」時下載頁面內容。這類連線的對象與紀錄由該網站決定，適用該網站自己的隱私權政策，開發者不參與也無法看到。

若你的照片存放在 iCloud 照片，iOS 可能在顯示、編輯或壓縮時從 iCloud 下載原始檔，這是 Apple 系統的行為，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。

iCloud 同步（選用，預設關閉）：你在「設定 › iCloud 同步」手動打開後，App 才會把自己的資料（你勾選的項目：紀錄〔文字、連結、分類〕、紀錄的圖片與錄音、整理與相簿的偏好設定、個人設定；各項皆可獨立開關，預設全部關閉）存放到你自己 Apple ID 的 iCloud 空間。資料只在你登入同一個 Apple ID 的裝置之間同步，存放在 Apple 的服務上，開發者無法讀取，也不會傳到我們的伺服器，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。關閉後 App 不再上傳；已在 iCloud 的檔案可在 iPhone「設定 › Apple ID › iCloud」管理或刪除。

5. 分享與匯出

只有在你按下「分享」「複製」「匯出」「送到 AI App」等按鈕時，內容才會交給你選擇的 App 或服務。交出去之後，由接收方的政策管理。「整合想法」不會自動上傳，只是把文字交給你選的 App。

6. 兒童

點滴相簿不收集任何人的資料，包括兒童。App 的分級依 App Store 年齡分級問卷填寫（因為可以開啟任意網址）。

7. 保存期間與刪除

我們沒有儲存你的資料，所以沒有需要我們代為刪除的資料。你可以隨時在 App 內刪除連結、想法或整理紀錄，或刪除 App 清除全部 App 內資料。在 App 內刪除的照片會先進入 iOS「最近刪除」，依系統設定保留一段時間後才會永久移除。

8. 政策更新

若日後功能改變而影響資料處理方式（例如加入 iCloud 同步），我們會先更新本頁並調整生效日期，再於 App 內提醒。

9. 聯絡我們

對本政策或 App 有任何疑問，請使用回報問題表單。

你也可以在 App 的「設定 › 關於 › 回報問題」填寫表單（表單連結）回報問題。表單內容由表單服務商處理，我們只用來回覆你並改善 App；請不要在表單中填寫密碼等敏感資料。

TidyCollect (點滴相簿) Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Applies to the app “TidyCollect” (Chinese name: 點滴相簿)

In short: your photos, links, ideas and sorting history stay on your iPhone. We run no servers, require no sign-up, collect no personal data, and use no ads or analytics.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system and includes no third-party SDKs (advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking). The developer cannot see your photos, your sorting results, or how you use the app.

2. What the app handles on your device

DataPurposeWhere it lives
Photos and videos (through the iOS Photos permission)Viewing, sorting, adding to albums, favoriting, editing, compressing, finding duplicates, and deleting at your requestStays in the system Photos library; the app does not upload it
Sorting records (keep / delete / pending marks, statistics, achievements)Remembering your progressThe app’s private storage
Links, ideas (text, photos, camera shots, videos, drawings) and categoriesCollections and notes you createThe app’s private storage; the Share Extension and the main app share it through an App Group
Settings (appearance, language, locked-album list, nickname)Saving your preferencesThe app’s private storage

This data stays on your device. We do not receive it and it is not sent to any server. Deleting the app removes the app’s own data (photos in your Photos library are unaffected, unless you deleted them inside the app).

3. Permissions

  • Photos: needed to sort your photos. Adding to albums, favoriting, editing and deleting modify your Photos library only when you ask. You can limit access to selected photos in Settings › TidyCollect › Photos.
  • Camera: used only when you choose “Take photo” in Ideas; the picture is saved inside the app as an idea card.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only when you use “Voice note” in Records. Recordings are stored compressed on your device. Speech-to-text is performed by iOS speech recognition; depending on language and device, audio may be sent to Apple servers and is subject to Apple’s privacy policy. The app does not send recordings to our servers.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used to unlock albums you have locked. Authentication is performed by iOS; the app never receives your face or fingerprint data.

4. Network connections

The app does not connect to any server operated by us. Only when you save a link does your device connect directly to the website you saved to fetch its title and preview image, or to download the page when you choose “Archive full page”. That website decides what it records and its own privacy policy applies; the developer is not involved and cannot see it.

If your photos are stored in iCloud Photos, iOS may download originals when you view, edit or compress them. This is Apple’s system behavior and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

iCloud Sync (optional, off by default): Only after you turn it on in Settings › iCloud Sync does the app store its own data (only the items you tick: log entries [text, links, categories], log images and recordings, sorting and album preferences, personal settings; each can be switched on or off separately, all off by default) in your own iCloud account. Data syncs only between devices signed in to the same Apple ID, is stored on Apple’s services, cannot be read by the developer, and is never sent to our servers. Apple’s privacy policy applies. Turning it off stops uploads; files already in iCloud can be managed or deleted in iPhone Settings › Apple ID › iCloud.

5. Sharing and export

Content leaves the app only when you tap actions such as Share, Copy, Export, or Send to an AI app, and only to the app or service you choose. After that, the recipient’s policy applies. “Merge ideas” never uploads anything by itself; it only hands text to the app you pick.

6. Children

TidyCollect collects no data from anyone, including children. The age rating follows the App Store questionnaire (the app can open arbitrary web addresses).

7. Retention and deletion

We store none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can delete links, ideas or sorting records inside the app at any time, or delete the app to remove all in-app data. Photos deleted inside the app first go to iOS “Recently Deleted” and are removed permanently after the period set by the system.

8. Changes

If a future feature changes how data is handled (for example iCloud sync), we will update this page and its effective date first, and notify you in the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: please use the report form.

You can also report a problem with the form (form link) or in the app (Settings › About › Report a problem). The form is processed by the form provider; we use it only to reply to you and improve the app. Please do not enter passwords or other sensitive data.
